Getting the best results

  • Set up behind you. Prop your phone on a stand or stable surface behind you, looking down the target line, with your whole body in frame.
  • Pick your club. Choose Irons, Woods or Driver at the top of the Practice screen. Each club is coached against tour pros swinging that club.
  • Good light helps. Daylight at an outdoor range gives the most reliable detection. Dim indoor or night lighting can cause missed swings.
  • Just swing. Swings are detected automatically. You'll hear a tip a few seconds after each one.
  • AirPods recommended. Tips play through connected headphones, or the phone speaker otherwise.

Common questions

A swing wasn't detected. Check that your whole body is in frame from setup to finish, and that nobody walks between you and the camera.

Replays have no sound. Turn on microphone access in Settings › Puro.

Do my videos get uploaded? No. Videos stay on your device. See the privacy policy.

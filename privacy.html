Privacy Policy

Puro (listed on the App Store as PuroGolf) · Effective October 30, 2026

Puro is an iPhone and iPad app that watches your golf swing through the camera and gives you a coaching tip after each swing. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: your videos stay on your device, there are no accounts, and the only thing that leaves your device automatically is anonymous swing-detection statistics.

No accounts

Puro does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any login. There is nothing to sign up for.

Camera and swing videos

  • Puro uses the camera to record your swing. Pose detection and swing analysis run entirely on your device.
  • Swing videos and replays are stored only inside the app on your device.
  • A video leaves your device only if you choose to save it to your Photos library or share it yourself.
  • We never upload, view or collect your videos or images.

Microphone

Puro uses the microphone to record the sound of your strike together with the swing video, so replays include audio. This audio is stored with the video on your device and is never uploaded.

Anonymous swing statistics

To improve how well Puro detects and analyzes swings, the app sends anonymous statistics to Apple's CloudKit service at the end of a practice session. This data includes:

  • Swing-detection results, for example whether a swing was detected, confidence values, swing-phase timings and scores.
  • Performance information, for example how long analysis took and the camera frame rate.
  • Your device model, iOS version and app version.
  • A random identifier created when the app is installed, used only to group one installation's sessions together.

This data does not include video, images, audio, your name, contact details, location, contacts, or the advertising identifier. It is not linked to your identity, and we cannot use it to identify you.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Puro contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Where data is stored and for how long

Videos, replays and your swing history stay on your device until you delete them or delete the app. Anonymous statistics are stored in Apple's CloudKit service and kept only as long as they are useful for improving swing detection. Because these statistics are anonymous, we cannot match them to a specific person. Deleting the app stops any further statistics from being sent.

Permissions

You can turn camera and microphone access on or off at any time in the iOS Settings app under Puro. Puro needs the camera to work. Without the microphone, replays will have no sound.

Children

Puro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
